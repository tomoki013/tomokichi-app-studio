title: At a distance, most days are the ones you don't meet
date: 2026-07-08

I had been counting how many days until we could meet. A number going down is easy to read. But counted that way, every day in between becomes "a day we still haven't met."

In reality those days were just life. Reading in a café, going to a museum, catching a sunset. Things I meant to talk about when we met, and had half forgotten by the time we did.

It seemed better if the days themselves stayed, not only the number.

Remeet came out of that. The days remaining are there, but they are not the whole screen. Photos and notes from the waiting time sit by date, and the things you want to do together sit next to them.

The two cities on the map, each with its own local time, are there for the same reason. Knowing what hour the other person is in makes the distance visible as something other than "N days left."

The day you meet goes into an album. Create the next reunion and it starts over.
