An empty day in my calendar came with something like guilt. If it is open, surely I could be doing something with it.

So the time I had meant to rest in ended up holding an errand. And after agreeing to it, I would notice I had not really wanted to. This happened more than once.

The problem was not a lack of time to rest. The time was there. It was that blank space is not treated as a plan, so it stays overwritable.

I tried writing "do nothing" down with a start and an end. Ten to ten thirty. That alone stopped me putting anything else there. Something written down is harder to move.

Yohaku came out of that. Not an app for managing plans — an app for holding on to the time you deliberately leave unplanned.

A few things were decided while building it. No timer during the space. No notification when it ends. I did not want to give anyone a reason to open the app during time meant for nothing. It says something once, shortly before the start, and then stays quiet.

Afterwards it asks once whether the space happened. Answering is optional. Close it without answering and it will not ask about that one again.
