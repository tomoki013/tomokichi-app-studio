This site used to hold only the apps I had finished. A name, one line of description, a screenshot. I thought that was enough.

What it left out was why any of them existed.

Almost nothing I make starts as an app. Something in ordinary life comes first. Getting tired from trying to make good use of time that had nothing in it. A long stretch of days without seeing someone. A flat where there was no line between the entrance and the room. I sit with it for a while, and only some of those turn into "it would be quicker to just build this."

Most of them do not. Some end at a walk that answered the question. Some end at a small test that showed it was the wrong shape. I wanted those in the same place too.

So I am adding a record to this site — putting what comes before an app next to the app itself. The order usually runs like this:

1. Notice something in daily life
2. Think about it, write it down
3. Try it small
4. Give it a form, if it needs one

Plenty of things stop before step four. They still get written down. Deciding not to build something is also a result.
